# Cortexa AI Quality & Governance

Cortexa treats AI quality as an operational workflow. Administrators can define repeatable RAG evaluation cases, execute them in background workers, inspect groundedness, expected-answer quality, citation matching, safe no-answer behavior, latency, and pass rates.

Users can rate completed AI responses as helpful or not helpful and provide structured issue reasons. Administrators review reported responses, add internal notes, and mark feedback as reviewed or resolved.

Enterprise analytics combine response reliability, evaluation results, feedback quality, citation coverage, latency, knowledge health, document usage, and model usage without exposing hidden reasoning or private retrieved passages.
